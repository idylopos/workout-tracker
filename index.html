Private by design

This app has no analytics, accounts, or database. It only loads public plan files from this site. Workout records and drafts stay in this browser and are never uploaded. Passphrase protection is optional; when enabled, records are encrypted before browser storage and the temporary unlock stores a non-exportable key, never the passphrase. Export a backup regularly and erase the app before handing off a shared device.
